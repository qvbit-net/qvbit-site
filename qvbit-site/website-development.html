How it works

Simple from start to launch

  1. We learn about your business. We collect your logo, photos, services, contact information, and goals.
  2. You own the accounts. We help you create Squarespace, Cloudflare, and GitHub accounts in your name.
  3. QVB I.T. builds the website. We design, configure, test, and connect the pieces.
  4. You review it. We make the approved changes before launch.
  5. You take control. You can make basic changes yourself or have QVB I.T. handle them for you.
Your choice after launch

Manage it yourself or let QVB I.T. help

We don't want you dependent on us just to change a phone number or update your hours. You can manage your site yourself. When you need a larger change, technical help, or simply don't have the time, we're here.
