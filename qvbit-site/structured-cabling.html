Structured Cabling & Physical Network Infrastructure

QVB I.T. supports the physical layer of business networks—from Ethernet drops and fiber to racks, patch panels, labeling, testing, and site-ready infrastructure. For work requiring a licensed specialty contractor, we coordinate with the appropriate qualified partner while keeping the project aligned with the overall network deployment.

Structured Cabling & Infrastructure Support

Services are scoped around the requirements of the network, site, and deployment schedule.

  • Cat5e, Cat6, and Cat6A Ethernet infrastructure
  • Fiber optic cabling and physical infrastructure support
  • Patch panels, racks, cabinets, labeling, and cleanup
  • Wi-Fi access point cabling and deployment support
  • Moves, adds, and changes for business networks
  • Cable testing, verification, and documentation
  • Coordination with qualified licensed partners when required
