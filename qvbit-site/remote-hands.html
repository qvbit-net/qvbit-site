Field Services & Remote Hands

When a technical task needs someone on-site, QVB I.T. provides field support for businesses, IT providers, data centers, offices, and infrastructure projects. Our technicians can work from a defined scope, coordinate with a remote engineer, document the work performed, and report site conditions.

Field Services

Typical assignments include:

  • Rack-and-stack, equipment installation, and swaps
  • Server, switch, firewall, and network-device support
  • Equipment moves, adds, changes, and decommissioning
  • Console access, power cycling, and physical troubleshooting
  • Site surveys, inventory verification, and photo documentation
  • Remote-engineer coordination and deployment support
  • Time-sensitive and scheduled field engineering work
